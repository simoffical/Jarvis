# Q1 Budget

The first-quarter financial plan for Ember & Oak Roastery, built by [[Sam Okafor]] with input from
[[Maria Chen]] on revenue targets and from Jules Tan on marketing spend for the Instagram Strategy
and Email Newsletter.

The Q1 Budget accounts for green coffee purchases from Green Valley Coffee Imports, Payroll for the
whole team, Probat Roaster maintenance, and the Lease Agreement payments on both properties.

Sam flags variances weekly and reports significant ones directly to Maria during the Tuesday ops
sync.
