# Payroll

Biweekly payroll for the Ember & Oak Roastery team, run by [[Sam Okafor]] and covering
[[Maria Chen]], Devon Ruiz, Priya Patel, Jules Tan, and the part-time barista staff.

Payroll is the largest recurring line item in the Q1 Budget and is reviewed each time the company
hires, most recently when Priya Patel brought on two additional baristas ahead of the Seasonal
Pumpkin Spice Latte launch.

Sam also handles contractor payments to Green Valley Coffee Imports logistics partners through the
same system.
