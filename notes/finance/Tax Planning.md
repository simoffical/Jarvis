# Tax Planning

Quarterly tax preparation and planning for Ember & Oak Roastery, managed by [[Sam Okafor]] with an
outside accountant. Draws on figures from the Q1 Budget, Payroll records, and Inventory Management
valuations for cost-of-goods-sold calculations.

Sam coordinates with [[Maria Chen]] on estimated payments and keeps documentation for every
Green Valley Coffee Imports shipment and Probat Roaster depreciation schedule.

Tax Planning also covers the sales tax collected at the Farmers Market Booth, which is tracked
separately from cafe register sales.
