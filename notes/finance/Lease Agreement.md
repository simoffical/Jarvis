# Lease Agreement

Covers both the Ember & Oak Roastery warehouse, where the Probat Roaster lives, and the retail cafe
space managed day-to-day by [[Priya Patel]]. Negotiated and renewed by [[Sam Okafor]] every three
years.

The Lease Agreement is one of the largest fixed costs in the Q1 Budget, and Maria Chen was directly
involved in the most recent renewal to secure a five-year rate lock.

Any expansion plans, including a second cafe location, will require renegotiating this Lease
Agreement first.
