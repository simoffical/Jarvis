# Pricing Strategy

The framework [[Maria Chen]] and [[Sam Okafor]] use to price every Ember & Oak Roastery product,
from the everyday Sunrise Blend to the premium Ethiopia Yirgacheffe.

Pricing Strategy accounts for green coffee costs from Green Valley Coffee Imports, labor time on the
Probat Roaster, and margin targets set in the Q1 Budget. Wholesale pricing for Midnight Espresso is
reviewed separately each summer.

Jules Tan checks new pricing against competitor cafes before any Instagram Strategy announcement.
