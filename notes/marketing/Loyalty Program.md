# Loyalty Program

A punch-card-turned-app loyalty system run by [[Priya Patel]] at the Ember & Oak cafe, rewarding
customers with a free bag of Sunrise Blend after ten purchases.

Jules Tan is piloting a referral tier that ties into the Instagram Strategy and Email Newsletter,
aiming to boost repeat visits ahead of the Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte launch each fall.

Sam Okafor tracks redemption costs against the Q1 Budget to make sure the program stays profitable.
