# Email Newsletter

A biweekly email from Ember & Oak Roastery written by [[Jules Tan]], covering new roasts, cafe
events like the Farmers Market Booth, and seasonal items such as the Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte
and Cold Brew Concentrate.

The Email Newsletter list is built through sign-ups on the Website Redesign and in-cafe prompts from
Priya Patel's team. Jules cross-promotes every issue on the Instagram Strategy and references
Loyalty Program milestones to encourage repeat purchases.

Maria Chen reviews the newsletter tone quarterly to keep it aligned with brand voice.
