# Instagram Strategy

Jules Tan's content plan for Ember & Oak Roastery's Instagram account, built around product
launches like the Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte and Cold Brew Concentrate, plus behind-the-scenes
looks at [[Devon Ruiz]] running the Probat Roaster.

The Instagram Strategy feeds directly into the Email Newsletter calendar and promotes appearances
at the Farmers Market Booth. Priya Patel supplies daily cafe photo content, and Maria Chen appears
in occasional founder-story posts.

Jules tracks engagement against goals set in the Q1 Budget marketing line.
