# Website Redesign

A 2024 project led by [[Jules Tan]] to rebuild the Ember & Oak Roastery website, adding online
ordering for the Sunrise Blend, Midnight Espresso, and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe alongside subscription
options.

The Website Redesign integrates with the Loyalty Program for account sign-ins and links out to the
Instagram Strategy and Email Newsletter for content. Maria Chen signed off on the final design after
three rounds of revisions.

Sam Okafor allocated the project budget as a one-time capital expense in the Q1 Budget.
