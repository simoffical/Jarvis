# Farmers Market Booth

A weekly Saturday booth Ember & Oak Roastery runs at the downtown farmers market, staffed on
rotation by [[Maria Chen]] and members of Priya Patel's cafe team.

The booth features tastings of the Ethiopia Yirgacheffe and bag sales of the Sunrise Blend and
Midnight Espresso, and doubles as market research for new product ideas.

Jules Tan promotes the booth's schedule through the Instagram Strategy and Email Newsletter each
week, and Sam Okafor tracks it as a distinct revenue line in the Q1 Budget.
