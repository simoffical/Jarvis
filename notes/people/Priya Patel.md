# Priya Patel

Cafe Manager overseeing the day-to-day front-of-house operations at the Ember & Oak flagship cafe.
Priya manages the barista team, schedules shifts, and is the primary owner of the Loyalty Program
rollout.

She coordinates with Devon Ruiz on bean freshness and rotation, and works with Jules Tan on the
Instagram Strategy to showcase daily specials like the Cold Brew Concentrate and the Seasonal
Pumpkin Spice Latte.

Priya also handles customer feedback and reports recurring complaints or praise back to
[[Maria Chen]] during the monthly ops review.
