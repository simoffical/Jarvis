# Maria Chen

Founder and CEO of Ember & Oak Roastery. Maria started roasting coffee in her garage in 2016 after
a decade working as a coffee buyer for a large importer. She oversees company vision, supplier
relationships, and works closely with [[Devon Ruiz]] on sourcing decisions.

Maria personally negotiated the standing contract with Green Valley Coffee Imports and sets the
overall Pricing Strategy each year alongside Sam Okafor. She is the public face of the brand and
often represents the roastery at the Farmers Market Booth and industry trade shows.

Key traits: relentless about quality, obsessed with the Ethiopia Yirgacheffe lot, and a firm
believer that the Roasting Process should never be rushed for volume's sake.
