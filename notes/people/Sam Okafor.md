# Sam Okafor

Bookkeeper and part-time financial controller for Ember & Oak Roastery. Sam prepares the Q1 Budget,
runs Payroll every two weeks, and manages the Lease Agreement renewal for both the roastery
warehouse and the retail cafe space.

Sam works directly with [[Maria Chen]] on the annual Pricing Strategy, modeling margins across the
Sunrise Blend, Midnight Espresso, and wholesale accounts. Sam is also responsible for Tax Planning
and keeps receipts for every Green Valley Coffee Imports shipment for cost-of-goods tracking.

Known around the office for the spreadsheet that predicted the cash crunch three months before it
happened.
