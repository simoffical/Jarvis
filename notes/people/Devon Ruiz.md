# Devon Ruiz

Head Roaster at Ember & Oak Roastery, hired by [[Maria Chen]] in 2018. Devon runs the Probat
Roaster daily and owns the Roasting Process documentation, including profiles for the Sunrise
Blend, Midnight Espresso, and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe.

Devon also maintains the Quality Control Checklist and trains new roasting apprentices. Before
joining the company, Devon competed in regional roasting championships and still brings that
obsessive attention to detail to every batch.

Works closely with Priya Patel to make sure the cafe always has fresh beans, and flags any
Inventory Management issues to Sam Okafor when green stock runs low.
