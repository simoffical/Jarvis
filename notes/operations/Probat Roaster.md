# Probat Roaster

The 25kg drum roaster that powers all production at Ember & Oak Roastery, operated daily by
[[Devon Ruiz]]. Purchased in 2019 as the company's largest capital expense, financed through terms
negotiated by Sam Okafor.

The Probat Roaster is central to the Roasting Process documentation and its maintenance schedule is
a line item in the Q1 Budget. Any downtime directly threatens Inventory Management targets for the
Sunrise Blend and Midnight Espresso.

Maria Chen still remembers the day it was installed as the moment the company stopped being a
garage operation.
