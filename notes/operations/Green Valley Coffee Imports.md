# Green Valley Coffee Imports

Ember & Oak Roastery's primary green coffee supplier, providing the raw beans behind the Sunrise
Blend, Midnight Espresso, and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe. [[Maria Chen]] negotiated the standing contract
in 2017 and still handles the relationship personally.

Green Valley ships pallets to the warehouse covered under the company's Lease Agreement, and every
shipment is logged for Inventory Management and cost tracking in the Q1 Budget by Sam Okafor.

Devon Ruiz does a cupping session on every new lot before it enters the regular Roasting Process
rotation.
