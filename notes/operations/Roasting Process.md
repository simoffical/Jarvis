# Roasting Process

Internal documentation of how Ember & Oak Roastery roasts every product, maintained by
[[Devon Ruiz]]. Covers profiles for the Sunrise Blend, Midnight Espresso, and Ethiopia Yirgacheffe,
each with target first-crack times and drop temperatures on the Probat Roaster.

The Roasting Process also defines the handoff to the Quality Control Checklist: no batch reaches
Inventory Management or the cafe floor without a passing cup score.

Maria Chen reviews this document annually to make sure it still reflects the company's quality
standards as the team scales production.
