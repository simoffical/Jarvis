# Inventory Management

The system Ember & Oak Roastery uses to track green coffee from Green Valley Coffee Imports,
roasted stock coming off the Probat Roaster, and finished bags ready for the cafe or wholesale.

[[Devon Ruiz]] updates roasted inventory daily, while Priya Patel tracks retail drawdown for the
Sunrise Blend, Midnight Espresso, and seasonal items like the Cold Brew Concentrate.

Sam Okafor reconciles Inventory Management numbers monthly against the Q1 Budget to catch shrinkage
early, a habit that started after the 2021 stock discrepancy scare.
