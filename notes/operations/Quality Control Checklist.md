# Quality Control Checklist

A step-by-step cupping and inspection checklist used after every roast, enforced by [[Devon Ruiz]]
as part of the Roasting Process. Each batch of Sunrise Blend, Midnight Espresso, or Ethiopia
Yirgacheffe must pass this checklist before Priya Patel's team can sell it in the cafe.

Failed batches are logged and excluded from Inventory Management counts, which Sam Okafor factors
into cost-of-goods calculations for the Q1 Budget.

Maria Chen introduced this checklist after a customer complaint in 2020 and treats it as
non-negotiable.
