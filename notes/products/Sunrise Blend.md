# Sunrise Blend

Ember & Oak Roastery's flagship medium roast, a blend of Colombian and Brazilian beans sourced
through Green Valley Coffee Imports. Roasted weekly by [[Devon Ruiz]] on the Probat Roaster
following a profile documented in the Roasting Process notes.

Sunrise Blend is the best-selling bag in the retail cafe and the top wholesale SKU. It anchors the
Pricing Strategy as the "everyday" tier, priced below the single-origin Ethiopia Yirgacheffe.

Featured heavily in the Instagram Strategy as the "start your morning right" product, and included
in every new customer's welcome kit under the Loyalty Program.
