# Cold Brew Concentrate

A bottled cold brew concentrate made from a custom blend, steeped for 18 hours and sold both in the
cafe and in local grocery stores. Priya Patel manages daily batching and inventory at the cafe
level.

Cold Brew Concentrate has become a strong summer seasonal seller, tracked separately in the Q1
Budget from hot beverage sales. Jules Tan runs targeted Instagram Strategy posts each May to kick
off the season.

Devon Ruiz sources the beans for this blend outside the normal Roasting Process rotation, using
slightly stale stock that would otherwise be discounted.
