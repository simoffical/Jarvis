# Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

A single-origin light roast that [[Maria Chen]] considers the soul of the company. Sourced in small
lots via Green Valley Coffee Imports directly from a cooperative Maria visited in 2019.

Ethiopia Yirgacheffe is roasted last each week on the Probat Roaster because Devon Ruiz insists the
machine needs to "cool its temper" after darker runs. It commands the highest price point in the
Pricing Strategy and is the star of the Farmers Market Booth tasting table.

Limited quantities mean Jules Tan features it sparingly in the Email Newsletter to preserve scarcity.
