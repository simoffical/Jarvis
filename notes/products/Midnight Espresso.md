# Midnight Espresso

A dark roast espresso blend built for the cafe's espresso bar, used in every drink Priya Patel's
barista team pulls, including the Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte base shot.

Midnight Espresso is roasted in small batches by [[Devon Ruiz]] to keep crema consistent, and its
formula is one of the few things Devon will not write down in full outside the internal Roasting
Process binder.

Wholesale accounts request Midnight Espresso more than any other product except the Sunrise Blend,
which keeps it central to the Inventory Management priorities each week.
