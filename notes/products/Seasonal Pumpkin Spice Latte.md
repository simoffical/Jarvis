# Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte

An autumn seasonal drink built on the Midnight Espresso base with house-made spice syrup, launched
every September by [[Priya Patel]]'s cafe team.

The Seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte is the single biggest driver of foot traffic in Q4 and gets its own
push in the Email Newsletter and Instagram Strategy campaigns from Jules Tan. Sam Okafor tracks its
revenue separately when building the following year's Q1 Budget.

Devon Ruiz jokes that this is the one drink where the espresso is basically a supporting actor.
